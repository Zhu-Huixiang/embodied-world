$\displaystyle \begin{gathered}
w_t(\theta)=\frac{\pi_\theta(a_t\mid s_t)}{\pi_{\theta_{\rm old}}(a_t\mid s_t)}\\[4pt]
L^{\rm CLIP}(\theta)=\mathbb E_t\Bigg[\\[-2pt]
\min\left\{\begin{aligned}
&w_t(\theta)\hat A_t,\\[2pt]
&\operatorname{clip}(w_t(\theta),1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)\hat A_t
\end{aligned}\right\}\Bigg]\\[5pt]
\max_\theta\;L^{\rm CLIP}(\theta)\\[3pt]
\epsilon=0.2
\end{gathered}$
